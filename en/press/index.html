Short description

BlockRacers is a free 3D racing game in your browser by BlockRacers Studio. Drive through Parkstad and Beekdaelen on real maps, with traffic. Create your avatar, upgrade your car in the garage, earn coins and find stunt spots. Level up and join multiple races with a leaderboard. Drive live with other logged-in players. Log in with email and password and your progress is saved. Add the game to your home screen, touch works. No ads, no third-party trackers, no free chat but set messages, and a report function. Now in an invite-only beta for 200 testers.
