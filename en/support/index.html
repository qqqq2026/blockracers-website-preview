PLAY THE BETA

Beta with 200 spots

Is it really free?

Yes! BlockRacers is free and there is nothing to buy.

Which devices can I play on?

In your browser, on phone, tablet or computer. Add the game to your home screen as an icon. Touchscreen works. A controller is not tested yet.

How do I start?

Create a free account. Spots left in the beta? Then you are approved automatically and click Play now on your account page. No code to enter. Create your avatar, check your garage and drive through Parkstad and Beekdaelen. Your progress is saved.

Is it safe for my child?

BlockRacers is made for kids. There is no free chat, only set messages. No ads and no third-party trackers. See something wrong? Report it in the game. More on Parents and privacy.

How do I report a bug?

Use the report button in the game and pick 'bug'.
