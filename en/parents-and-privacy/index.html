Short and honest

BlockRacers is an invite-only beta for ages 8+. Players drive through Parkstad and Beekdaelen, race and upgrade their car. No free chat, no ads, no third-party trackers.

What data do we store?

The game runs on Supabase, in the EU (Frankfurt).

  • Account: email address, when you log in and technical session data.
  • Profile, visible to other players: nickname (max 16 characters, no real names), avatar, coins, level, best times and dates.
  • Saves, only for you: progress, garage, upgrades and settings.
  • Reports: who reports whom, a reason (name, behaviour, bug, other), a short note of max 300 characters, status and date.
  • Live driving: your position is sent live to other players and is not stored.

Signing up for an invite

We store your email address and your consent. Under 16 also your parent's email address, because the invite goes to the parent. Only for the beta invite, stored in the EU. Delete your account and we delete your sign-up too.

Email

We send invites and Forgot password codes by email through Resend.

What can players do with each other?

Drive together in the world. Only logged-in players who are not banned by the studio can join. There is no free chat, only set messages to pick from.

Safety

See something wrong? Report a player in the game. Pick a reason: name, behaviour, bug or other. The studio reviews reports and can hide or ban players.

Deleting data

In the game: Start > Danger zone > Delete account, confirm with VERWIJDER. Your profile, cloud progress and your reports are deleted right away. See the Account page.

Purchases and ads

There are no purchases in the game. No ads and no third-party trackers.

Cookies and trackers

This website uses no analytics and no third-party trackers.

What can you do as a parent?

Want to see or delete your data? Delete your account in the game.

  • Play together with your child once.
  • Talk about what your child does online.
  • See something bad? Report it in the game.
